Capacity Allocation for 3D Gaussian Reconstruction

Ongoing research · static and dynamic scenes

Using FastGS as a starting point, I built controlled comparisons around Gaussian densification in static scenes and explored a separate extension to multi-view dynamic video. The static results are encouraging but preliminary; the dynamic results are mixed. I am now checking reproducibility and measuring the quality–cost trade-off.
