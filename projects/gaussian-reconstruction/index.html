Ongoing research

Capacity Allocation for 3D Gaussian Reconstruction

How can a 3D Gaussian Splatting system make better use of its representation capacity during reconstruction? I study this question through controlled experiments, starting from FastGS.

Starting point

FastGS

Settings

Static scenes · multi-view dynamic video

Stage

Preliminary experiments and validation

What I have done

01

Static reconstruction

I built controlled FastGS comparisons around decisions made during Gaussian densification. The results so far provide encouraging preliminary evidence, with broader validation still needed.

02

Multi-view dynamic video

I explored a separate selection mechanism for dynamic scenes, using matched baselines, event-level audits, and per-frame evaluation. Its outcomes are mixed, so I do not treat it as a confirmed improvement.

03

Reproducibility and cost

I am tracing each comparison back to its run artifacts, testing independent training seeds, and measuring training and representation costs before making broader claims.

Current interpretation

The static comparisons are promising within the settings tested. The dynamic experiments also show that a useful selection signal does not automatically improve the final reconstruction. Independent training runs, broader scene coverage, and a consistent quality–cost comparison are the next checks.

This project is in progress. The observations above are preliminary research findings, not a claim of general improvement or end-to-end acceleration.
